The problem

Most loyalty programs only know one thing about a customer: their points balance.

Generic tiers. Blanket discounts. No idea who's about to churn, who's worth a better offer, or which "loyal" account is actually a bot farming your signup bonus. That's not a loyalty program — that's a ledger.

The Ledgerly way

A loyalty engine with a brain, not just a balance.

Every member gets scored, segmented, and served the right reward automatically. Every transaction gets checked for anomalies in real time. Every merchant gets a dashboard that tells them who to worry about before revenue walks out the door.

The AI layer

Three models, working on every account, all the time.

①

Personalized recommendations

Ranks the reward catalog per member from real redemption and purchase history — so the offer they see is the one they'll actually redeem.

②

Churn risk scoring

RFM-based scoring surfaces which members are drifting away, weeks before they'd show up as a lost customer in your revenue report.

③

Fraud & anomaly detection

Flags abnormal earn velocity and outsized transactions automatically — catching points abuse before it drains your reward budget.
