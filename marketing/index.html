The problem

Your POS or CRM can tell you what happened. It can't tell you what's about to happen.

Whatever you use to track customers — a CRM, a POS, or a stamp-card app like Square Loyalty, Loyalzoo, Stamp Me — is good at recording what happened. None of them tell you who's drifting away, who's worth a better offer, or which "regular" is actually farming your signup bonus. That's not insight — that's a ledger.

The Ledgerly way

Keep the tools you have. Add the brain they're missing.

Bring your existing customer and transaction history — from a CRM, a POS, or a loyalty app — and every customer gets scored, segmented, and matched to the offer they're actually likely to redeem. Every transaction gets checked for anomalies. You get a dashboard that tells you who to worry about before revenue walks out the door — without ripping out whatever's already at your till.

The AI layer

Five models, working on every account, all the time.

①

Personalised recommendations

Ranks the reward catalog per member from real redemption and purchase history — so the offer they see is the one they'll actually redeem.

②

Churn risk scoring

RFM-based scoring surfaces which members are drifting away, weeks before they'd show up as a lost customer in your revenue report.

③

Fraud & anomaly detection

Flags abnormal earn velocity and outsized transactions automatically — catching points abuse before it drains your reward budget.

④

Future value forecasting

Upload your customer and transaction history and Ledgerly backtests a lifetime-value model per member — so you know who's worth investing in before you spend the budget.

⑤

Next-best-product picks

Item-based collaborative filtering over purchase history surfaces the single most likely next purchase for every member — a ready-made upsell list, not a guess.
